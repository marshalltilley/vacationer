Phase 1 · Jul 2 — 5

Florence

Uffizi Gallery Jul 3 AMThe great Renaissance collection — Botticelli, Leonardo, Raphael.
Galleria dell'Accademia Jul 3 PMHome of Michelangelo's David.
Brunelleschi's Dome Jul 2 or 3Non-refClimb inside the Duomo's dome to the lantern for city views.
Piazza della Signoria & Palazzo VecchioOur doorstep — the civic square, its statues, and the town-hall fortress.
Ponte VecchioMedieval bridge lined with goldsmiths' shops.
OltrarnoArtisan-workshop quarter across the river; good for evening wandering.

Phase 2 · Jul 5 — 10

Rome

Trevi · Pantheon · Piazza Navona Jul 5Historic-center walk: the fountain, the 2,000-year-old domed temple, the Baroque piazza.

St. Peter's Basilica Jul 6The largest church in Christendom; Michelangelo's Pietà.
Castel Sant'Angelo Jul 6Hadrian's tomb turned papal fortress, on the Tiber.
Janiculum Hill → Trastevere Jul 6Sunset panorama, then down into cobbled Trastevere for dinner.
Colosseum · Roman Forum · Palatine Jul 7The amphitheater, the ancient city center, and the emperors' hill.
Via di Monte Tarpeo & Campidoglio Jul 7The best free view over the Forum; Michelangelo's hilltop piazza.
Jewish Ghetto Jul 7One of Europe's oldest Jewish communities, around the Portico d'Ottavia.
Galleria Borghese Jul 8Bernini sculptures and Caravaggios in a villa; timed entry.
Pincio & Piazza del Popolo Jul 8Park-terrace overlook; twin-church piazza with Caravaggios in Santa Maria del Popolo.
Villa Medici Jul 8Non-refGuided tour of the French Academy's Renaissance villa and gardens.
Palazzo Zuccari & Spanish Steps Jul 8The "House of Monsters" doorway shaped like a mouth; the famous steps.
Basilica di San Clemente Jul 9Three layers: a medieval church over an early church over a Roman temple.
Appian Way e-bike Jul 9Ride the ancient Roman road past tombs and aqueducts, ~2–2.5 h.
CatacombsOptionalUnderground early-Christian burial tunnels along the Appian Way.
Aventine · Orange Garden · Keyhole Jul 9Quiet hilltop, orange-tree terrace, and St. Peter's framed through the Knights of Malta keyhole.

One major ticketed site per day, with a hotel rest in the afternoon. Jul 5 and Jul 9 deliberately add no other big museum or dig.

Conditions
What to expect
  • July heat — long walking days on stone
  • Rome is the hard-walking stretch; build in hotel rest
  • Coast days are sea, sun and swimming
  • General guidance — check the forecast the week before.
Rules to dress for
Churches & sites
  • Covered shoulders and knees for St. Peter's, the Vatican and cathedrals
  • Pompeii: small daypack only (under 30 × 30 × 15 cm)
  • One nicer outfit each for rooftop or polished dinners
Footwear
Shoes
  • Broken-in walking shoes · primary
  • Sandals for the coast and boat day
  • Cobblestones and ruins — nothing fragile.
Gear
Small kit
  • Swimwear + quick-dry towel for the boat day
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses, hat
  • Refillable water bottle
  • Light scarf or layer for churches
